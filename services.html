How It Works.

What exactly is included in the three layers?

+

Layer 1 is your dedicated VA team — pre-trained, pre-tested, and production-ready before they touch your account. Layer 2 is management and QA — a dedicated ops manager runs quality checks, weekly Pulse Reports, and handles all people management. Layer 3 is AI automation — we identify repetitive tasks and automate them so your team works on what actually moves the needle.

What tasks can VAs handle for my SEO agency?

+

Everything that keeps you stuck in fulfillment: GMB management, citation building, content uploads, report generation, client communication, link prospecting, technical audits, and more. If it can be documented, it can be delegated. We also layer in AI automation to eliminate the repetitive work entirely.

How does the onboarding process work?

+

You record a few Loom videos of how you do things today. We audit those recordings, redesign the workflow for 50% more efficiency, document everything into hardened SOPs, and deploy a VA who has already shadowed on those processes. Most deployments happen within 7–14 days. No drawn-out onboarding.

What happens if my VA leaves or needs replacement?

+

We replace them immediately at no cost. Your SOPs are hardened and independent of any single person, so the next VA picks up exactly where the last one left off. Backup VAs are pre-trained on your processes before day one. You never retrain. That is the entire point of our Production-Ready Standard.

How does the AI automation layer work?

+

We audit every workflow your VAs run and identify tasks that can be fully or partially automated. Things like data entry, report formatting, status updates, and repetitive lookups get handed to AI. Your VAs focus on the work that requires judgment and nuance. Our longest client started with 15 VAs — we automated many of those roles, increasing their margins even further.

What is a Friday Pulse Report?

+

Every Friday, you get a report showing exactly what your team accomplished, what is in progress, and what needs your attention. No chasing updates. No wondering if work got done. It is full visibility into your operations without you having to manage anything.
