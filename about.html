From Call Centers to Scaling Agencies.

How a team of Filipino operators became the backbone behind scaling SEO agencies.

The Origin Story
The Beginning
I grew up poor in the Philippines. My mother left for Hong Kong as a domestic helper when I was in 4th grade. I graduated class valedictorian with 13 gold medals. During my Grade 5 awards ceremony, no family member came to pin my medal. A stranger did it. That moment shaped everything that followed.
The Grind
I worked in call centers for nearly 10 years. Customer service, sales, technical support, IT helpdesk. Always chasing the next pay increase. I dropped out of engineering twice because my family could not afford the living expenses. The same dream was pulled away, twice.
2015
A friend mentioned working online. It sounded like a scam. I quit my call center job, bought a laptop, got internet installed, and landed my first client in under a week. Within months I had three clients. That year I started working with Bradley Benner, who became my longest client and my business school.
2017
I went travelling to Europe. Seeing how businesses operated in different countries sparked something. I started envisioning building an agency, not just freelancing for clients.
2018
I registered the business and studied web development. That same year I met my current dev lead. I started hiring and training VAs to support my clients. But I realized something: VAs alone were not the answer. Agencies needed systems. They needed structure. They needed continuity. So I built that.
2019
I met my now head of operations. The team started to take shape. With the right people in place, the vision shifted from just providing VAs to building an entire operational backbone for agencies.
2020
The pandemic hit while I was travelling in India. I got trapped there for months. It was a setback, but the business kept running. I used that time to study blogging and content strategy, adding another layer to what I could offer clients.
2022
Bradley massively scaled his services. We went from 2-3 VAs to 15+ in three months. We learned something crucial: when you have processes, structures, and systems, you can scale. When you do not, you are just hiring chaos. That was our proof of concept.
2023
I started investing in myself and hired coaches. We helped Bradley grow from 1 company to 3 companies. I realized I was not just managing VAs anymore. I was building infrastructure.
2024
I went to business conventions and first discovered AI. The possibilities hit me immediately. I saw how automation could eliminate the repetitive work that burned out VAs and slowed agencies down.
2025
I heavily invested in AI and built my AI dev team. Bradley reached $1M+ in revenue and we started automating his workflows. We proved that the right operational system can transform an agency. Now we are building that system for you.

About the Team.

Where are your VAs based?

+

The Philippines. Our team works remotely with total schedule flexibility, which is why they stay. We pay above-market rates, provide career growth paths, and treat our VAs as long-term team members — not disposable labor. Happy VAs do not ghost.

How do you train your VAs?

+

Every VA completes our Three-Pillar Training before deployment: VA Basic Skills (tools, communication, time management), Corporate US Culture (how American businesses operate), and AI Tools (prompt engineering, automation workflows). Then they shadow our senior team on real client accounts. You are never their guinea pig.

Who manages the VAs day to day?

+

We do. A dedicated ops manager handles QA, performance reviews, task management, and weekly reporting. You get a Friday Pulse Report showing exactly what got done. You set the priorities — we handle the people management.

Why do your VAs stay when others quit?

+

We pay more than the market rate, offer total schedule flexibility, and invest in their career growth. Our VAs are not bouncing between gigs on Upwork — they are building careers. When people feel valued and fairly paid, they stay. And when they stay, your operations stay stable.

How long has your team been doing this?

+

We started in call centers and evolved into building operational backbones for scaling SEO agencies. Our longest client has been with us for years — starting with 15 VAs, then scaling down as we automated roles with AI. We have seen every failure mode and built systems to prevent them.
