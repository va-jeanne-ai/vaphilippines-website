3. For **LinkedIn posts**, use 1200x627 landscape format

### Brand consistency tips
- Always start with the base style prefix for consistency
- Add your logo as a watermark in post-production (bottom-right corner)
- Keep the dark background + neon glow aesthetic across ALL visuals
- Use Orbitron font for titles, Inter for body text (add in Canva/Figma after generation)
- Primary glow should always be teal `#14B8A6` or cyan `#00FFFF`

### Logo placement
- **Instagram:** Logo watermark bottom-right, 15% opacity
- **LinkedIn:** Logo in header image or bottom-right corner
- **X:** Logo as profile picture, no watermark needed on images
